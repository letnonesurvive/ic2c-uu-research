# Changelog

## 1.0.0 — first release

- UU Research Station (MV machine): pick a ghost target, feed UU-Matter one unit at a time, get the first
  craft and unlock the recipe for the whole world.
- UU-Matter recipes are hidden in JEI and cannot be crafted until researched (configurable).
- Researched recipes panel in the machine window; item tooltips show research status and cost (Shift).
- Iridium ore is part of the research; emerald and shulker shell recipes stay regular IC2 secrets.
- Upgrades (overclocker, transformer, energy storage, ejectors, muffler), comparator output, working sound.
- Admin commands `/uuresearch learn|forget|learnall|reset|list`.
